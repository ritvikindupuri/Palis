# Palis

Name inspired by palisade: a protective boundary. An original folded-perimeter symbol uses three offset forms for visibility, boundaries and the three specialist agents. Flat mint, ice and slate-blue colors remain legible on dark dashboards.

Assets: palis-icon.svg and palis-logo.svg. Original vector artwork; not a Palo Alto Networks or Microsoft mark. This is an app identity, not a trademark availability determination.
